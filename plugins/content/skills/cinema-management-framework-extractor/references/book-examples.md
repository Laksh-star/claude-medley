- Level of detail in scene descriptions
- Balance of dialogue vs action vs visual analysis

**Closing Style:**

- Do you circle back to opening scene/question?

**Distinctive Elements:**
- What makes your analysis uniquely yours?
- Signature phrases or approaches
- Things you always do or never do
- Your secret sauce
