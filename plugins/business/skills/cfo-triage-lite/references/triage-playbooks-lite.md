# 5-Fuse Triage Playbooks

Fuse-specific action sequences. Each playbook follows the 5-Fuse protocol: classify the Fuse Score → identify The One Thing → sequence 5–7 steps across This Week / This Month / This Quarter.

---

## Fuse 1: Liquidity

**Definition**: Cash on hand is insufficient to meet obligations in the next 30–60 days. The business may be profitable — this is a timing fuse, not a model fuse.

**Fuse Score**:
- **RED (< 2 weeks runway)**: Fuse is burning. Immediate cash infusion required. Some payments will be missed.
- **AMBER (2–6 weeks runway)**: Fuse is lit. Manageable with aggressive action starting today.
- **GREEN (6–12 weeks runway)**: Fuse is exposed. Thin buffer — one shock lights it. Focus on structural fixes.

**The One Thing**: Accelerate the single largest receivable collectible within 7 days. Identify by name, amount, and contact — not "improve collections."

**5-Fuse Action Sequence**:

| Timeframe | Action | Owner | Expected Impact |
|-----------|--------|-------|----------------|
| This Week | Identify and personally call top 3 overdue receivables (promoter call, not accounts team) | Promoter | Accelerate ₹X in receipts within 7–10 days |
| This Week | List all payables by due date. Which can be deferred 15–30 days without relationship damage? | Finance/Promoter | Defer ₹Y in outflows |
| This Week | Check CC/OD drawing power — unutilised limit? Draw if needed. | Finance | Immediate liquidity of ₹Z |
| This Month | Renegotiate payment terms with top 5 vendors — ask for 15-day extension | Operations | Structural improvement in payable days |
| This Month | Implement 7-day receivable follow-up cadence (weekly, not monthly) | Finance | Reduce receivable days by 10–15 |
| This Month | Freeze all non-essential capex for 90 days | Promoter | Preserve cash |
| This Quarter | Set up weekly cash position report (every Monday: bank balance, receivables due, payables due) | Finance | Permanent visibility — prevents Fuse 1 from relighting |

**Structural Root Cause**: "The 5-Fuse Triage identifies: your business is profitable but cash-poor because [receivables too slow / payables too fast / no buffer / seasonal mismatch]. Fixing the immediate fuse without fixing this structure means Fuse 1 relights in [X] months."

## Fuse 2: Solvency

**Definition**: Debt service exceeds operating cash flow capacity. This is a longer fuse than liquidity but more dangerous — it's structural, not timing.

**Fuse Score**:
- **RED (DSCR < 0.8)**: Fuse is burning. Cannot service debt from operations. Default approaching without restructuring.
- **AMBER (DSCR 0.8–1.1)**: Fuse is lit. Barely servicing. Any revenue dip triggers default.
- **GREEN (DSCR 1.1–1.25)**: Fuse is exposed. Heavy but serviceable. Deleverage before the next shock lights it.

**DSCR estimation** (if exact numbers unavailable): Monthly operating profit ÷ monthly total EMI + interest payments. Guide the user: "What's your monthly profit after all expenses except loan payments? And your total monthly loan repayment including interest?" Divide the first by the second — that's your approximate Fuse 2 score.

**The One Thing**: Contact primary lender and initiate restructuring conversation BEFORE a payment is missed. Banks have restructuring mechanisms; surprise default is the worst outcome.

**5-Fuse Action Sequence**:

| Timeframe | Action | Owner | Expected Impact |
|-----------|--------|-------|----------------|
| This Week | Calculate exact monthly debt service (all loans) vs. monthly operating cash flow | Finance/CA | Clarity on the Fuse 2 gap |
| This Week | If DSCR < 1.0, contact bank RM about restructuring (moratorium, tenure extension, rate renegotiation) | Promoter | Restructuring extends the fuse by 6–12 months |
| This Week | Stop all new borrowing immediately | Promoter | Prevent shortening the fuse further |
| This Month | Prepare 12-month cash flow projection showing restructured scenario | Finance/CA | Bank needs this for restructuring approval |
| This Month | Identify and exit unprofitable revenue streams — revenue not covering direct costs + debt service is negative value | Promoter | Sometimes shrinking revenue improves solvency |
| This Quarter | Create 24-month deleveraging plan with monthly milestones | Finance/Advisor | Structural path to DSCR > 1.5 — Fuse 2 defused |

**When to escalate**: Fuse Score RED and total debt > ₹2Cr → strongly recommend engaging a CA firm with restructuring experience. Beyond DIY territory.

**Structural Root Cause**: "The 5-Fuse Triage identifies: the business took on debt sized for [revenue/margin] that didn't materialise. The debt is now sized for a business you don't have. Options: restructure the debt, grow into it, or reduce it through asset sales / equity."

## Fuse 3: Revenue Cliff

**Definition**: Revenue concentration is high (top 3 clients > 60%) and one or more is at risk of reducing or leaving. This fuse can detonate into Fuse 1 (liquidity) within weeks.

**Fuse Score**:
- **RED (>40% from single client at risk)**: Existential fuse. Loss triggers immediate Fuse 1.
- **AMBER (>60% from top 3, one signaling reduction)**: Serious exposure. Fuse is lit.
- **GREEN (>60% from top 3, stable but concentrated)**: Structural vulnerability. One shock lights it.

**The One Thing**: Calculate the exact cash impact: monthly revenue loss × months to replace. This number determines whether to cut costs or invest in replacement revenue. Don't guess — calculate.

**5-Fuse Action Sequence**:

| Timeframe | Action | Owner | Expected Impact |
|-----------|--------|-------|----------------|
| This Week | Quantify: if [at-risk client] exits, what's the monthly revenue AND cash flow impact? (Factor in associated costs that drop too.) | Finance/Promoter | Real number, not feared number |
| This Week | Assess realistic replacement timeline. 3 months? 6? 12? Be honest. | Promoter/Sales | Determines the cash bridge needed |
| This Week | Understand WHY: call the decision-maker. Is it price? Service? Their own contraction? | Promoter | Sometimes the fuse can be extinguished, not just survived |
| This Month | Offer a restructured engagement at lower cost that keeps key deliverables. Half the revenue > zero. | Promoter | Preserve relationship — lengthen the fuse |
| This Month | Activate pipeline: 3–5 warm introductions through existing network. Not cold outreach. | Promoter/Sales | Begin replacement revenue pipeline |
| This Month | Build 6-month cash bridge plan (reserves + cost cuts + credit facility) | Finance | Preparedness if Fuse 3 detonates into Fuse 1 |
| This Quarter | Set concentration policy: no single client > 25% of revenue, no top 3 > 50%. | Promoter | Structural defusal — prevents Fuse 3 from recurring |

**Cliff mitigation before accepting the loss**: Negotiate transition timeline ("phase over 3 months instead of next month"). Cross-sell adjacent services to different part of client org. Buying 60 extra days can be the difference between a controlled transition and an emergency.

**Structural Root Cause**: "The 5-Fuse Triage identifies: revenue concentration isn't a financial problem — it's a business model problem. You've built a business that serves [1–3] clients well instead of a market. The financial fix is necessary but insufficient — the real defusal is building a sales engine that generates diversified demand."

## Fuse 4: Receivables

**Definition**: Business is profitable on paper but cash is trapped in outstanding invoices. Receivable days > 60, receivables > 3 months of revenue. A slow-burning fuse that can cascade into Fuse 1.

**Fuse Score**:
- **RED (Receivable days > 90, major client overdue)**: Cash starvation. May trigger secondary Fuse 1 crisis.
- **AMBER (Receivable days 60–90)**: Chronic but manageable with discipline.
- **GREEN (Receivable days 45–60, trending up)**: Early warning. Fix before it compounds into RED.

**The One Thing**: Identify the 3 largest overdue invoices by value and call each client's decision-maker (not accounts payable) this week.

**5-Fuse Action Sequence**:

| Timeframe | Action | Owner | Expected Impact |
|-----------|--------|-------|----------------|
| This Week | List all receivables by age and amount. Top 10 overdue invoices = the action list. | Finance | Visibility into where cash is trapped |
| This Week | Promoter personally calls top 3 overdue clients. Ask: "Is there an issue? What do we need to resolve to get this paid?" | Promoter | Personal attention accelerates payment |
| This Week | For invoices > 90 days: send formal demand notice (CA letterhead). This signals seriousness. | CA/Finance | Legal escalation path begins |
| This Month | Implement invoice-on-delivery policy for all new work. No more net-60. | Finance/Sales | Prevent future Fuse 4 accumulation |
| This Month | If registered on Udyam (MSME), cite Section 15 of MSMED Act for buyers who owe > 45 days | Finance/CA | Legal leverage for MSMEs |
| This Quarter | Set up a credit policy: credit terms by client tier (new clients: advance/COD; established: net-30 max) | Promoter/Finance | Structural defusal of Fuse 4 |

**Structural Root Cause**: "The 5-Fuse Triage identifies: you've been financing your clients' cash flow. Every day your invoice sits unpaid, you're giving them a free loan. The fix isn't better follow-up — it's setting terms that don't require heroic collection efforts."

## Fuse 5: Profitability

**Definition**: Revenue present but not making money. EBITDA negative or near-zero for 2+ quarters. The longest fuse — but the hardest to defuse because it's the business model itself.

**Fuse Score**:
- **RED (Negative EBITDA, 2+ quarters)**: Consuming capital. Every month shortens Fuse 1 by proxy.
- **AMBER (EBITDA 0–3%, declining)**: Marginally profitable, trending toward loss. One input cost increase or client loss pushes it RED.
- **GREEN (EBITDA 3–8%, below industry)**: Profitable but underperforming. The fuse exists but isn't lit.

**The One Thing**: Identify the single largest cost line reducible without destroying revenue in 30 days. Often: excess office space, underutilised staff, or above-market vendor contracts.

**5-Fuse Action Sequence**:

| Timeframe | Action | Owner | Expected Impact |
|-----------|--------|-------|----------------|
| This Week | Build product/service-level P&L. Which offerings are profitable and which aren't? | Finance/Promoter | Often reveals 20–30% of revenue is margin-negative |
| This Week | List all fixed costs. For each: "If eliminated, does revenue drop?" If no → reduction candidate. | Promoter | Identifies reducible fixed costs |
| This Month | Raise prices on lowest-margin offerings by 10–15%. Clients who leave were the unprofitable ones. | Promoter/Sales | Test pricing power — often, resistance is imagined |
| This Month | Renegotiate top 5 vendor contracts — get 2 competing quotes | Operations | 5–10% cost reduction on major inputs |
| This Quarter | Exit margin-negative offerings. Redirect resources to margin-positive ones. | Promoter | Portfolio-level margin improvement |
| This Quarter | Set margin floors: "We don't take work below X% gross margin." Communicate to sales. | Promoter | Prevents future Fuse 5 erosion |

**Structural Root Cause**: "The 5-Fuse Triage identifies: your profitability problem is [pricing weakness / cost mismatch / product mix / scale insufficiency]. You're [charging too little / spending too much / selling wrong things / too small for your cost base]. The fix is [X], not just 'cut costs.'"

## General 5-Fuse Triage Principles

1. **The One Thing First**: Every fuse has one action that buys the most time. Highlight it. Don't bury it in a list.
2. **Sequence by Timeframe**: This Week = promoter personally. This Month = involves others. This Quarter = structural defusal.
3. **Name the Owner**: "The promoter personally calls Client A's CFO this Wednesday" — not "the team should improve collections."
4. **Quantify Everything**: "Collect ₹12L from Invoice #4523 from Client X by Friday" — not "improve cash flow."
5. **Bridge to DB Health Score**: Always end with the structural root cause and: "This stops the bleeding. A full **DB Health Score** would reveal whether this is a one-time fuse or a structural pattern across all six dimensions."
6. **Honesty Over Comfort**: If the business model is broken (Fuse 5 RED for 3+ quarters with declining revenue), say so: "The 5-Fuse Triage shows this isn't a cash flow timing issue — the business isn't viable in its current form. We need a different conversation."
7. **Personal Finance Alert**: If promoter is funding personal from business (or vice versa), flag it: "This distorts your true Fuse Scores. Here's what the business looks like on its own."

## Cascade Warnings

Some fuses, when they detonate, light others. Flag these cascades explicitly:

- **Fuse 3 (Revenue Cliff) → Fuse 1 (Liquidity)**: "If [client] exits, your Fuse 1 score drops from GREEN to RED within [X] weeks. Build the cash bridge now."
- **Fuse 4 (Receivables) → Fuse 1 (Liquidity)**: "Your receivables backlog is slowly draining Fuse 1. Every week of delay shortens your runway."
- **Fuse 5 (Profitability) → Fuse 2 (Solvency)**: "Sustained margin erosion means you're servicing debt from shrinking cash flow. Fuse 2 gets shorter every quarter."
- **Fuse 1 (Liquidity) → Fuse 2 (Solvency)**: "A missed payment triggers NPA classification, which freezes your credit facilities and lights Fuse 2."

## The Shortest Fuse Rule — Multi-Crisis Protocol

When the 5-Fuse intake reveals two or more active fuses:

1. **Identify all active fuses** with their Fuse Scores
2. **Apply The Shortest Fuse Rule** — triage by fuse number (Fuse 1 first, always)
3. **Run the primary fuse's This Week actions**
4. **Merge secondary fuse actions** into This Month and This Quarter
5. **Present ONE unified 5-Fuse Triage Plan** — not separate playbooks

**Framing**: "The 5-Fuse Triage identifies two active fuses. **Fuse [X]: [Type]** (Fuse Score: [RED/AMBER]) is the shorter fuse — [timeline]. We cut that wire this week. **Fuse [Y]: [Type]** (Fuse Score: [RED/AMBER]) is the larger structural issue — [impact]. We address that this month. Here's the unified plan."

## Industry-Specific Fuse Calibration

### IT Services / Consulting
- **Fuse 1 and 3 are tightly coupled**: Client scope cuts create bench costs, which drain cash. Always calculate bench burn rate separately.
- **Redeployment before termination**: Indian notice periods (1–3 months) add lag to cost-cutting. Factor into Fuse 1 runway.
- **Scope negotiation extends Fuse 3**: Before accepting a 50% cut, explore reduced-scope engagement.

### Manufacturing / Engineering
- **Fuse 1 is stubborn**: Fixed costs (rent, power, machine EMIs) don't flex with revenue. Cost cuts limited to overtime, temp labour, discretionary maintenance.
- **Inventory as emergency Fuse 1 relief**: Slow-moving inventory can sometimes be liquidated at discount.

### Trading / Distribution
- **Fuse 5 detonates fast**: 5–10% margins mean small cost increases or price cuts flip profitability overnight.
- **Supplier relationships are Fuse 1 insurance**: First call in a liquidity crisis = key suppliers for extended terms.
